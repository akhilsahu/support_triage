# Yullo Core Terminology

This document defines the locked vocabulary for the Yullo product to ensure a clean, coherent, and consistent product hierarchy.

## 1. Yullo Link
**Definition:** The public address where a user's presence lives.
**Example:** `yullo.co/northstarstudio`
**Usage:** Use instead of "public page," "profile URL," or "site."

## 2. Aura
**Definition:** The intelligent experience behind the Yullo Link. It understands context, answers, guides, and connects.
**Usage:** Use instead of "bot," "assistant," "RAG," "semantic router," or "AI."

## 3. Home
**Definition:** The logged-in starting point for the user. Displays recent activity, Aura status, connections, and suggested actions.
**Usage:** Use instead of "dashboard" or "feed."

## 4. Space
**Definition:** The owner-facing place to edit and manage their presence. This includes editing their profile, appearance, sources, Aura settings, and link settings.
**Usage:** Use instead of "studio," "settings," or "editor."

## 5. Network
**Definition:** The discovery and connection layer across Yullo.
**Usage:** Represents the broader ecosystem of connected Yullo Links and users.

---

## The Product Hierarchy
This vocabulary creates a natural, easy-to-understand product flow:

You start at **Home**  
→ manage things in your **Space**  
→ which is powered by your **Aura**  
→ shared through your **Yullo Link**  
→ and connected through the **Network**.

## Post-Launch Actions
When a user launches their presence, the primary actions are:
- **View my Yullo Link** (Primary)
- **Go to Home** (Secondary)
- **Copy link** (Tertiary)
